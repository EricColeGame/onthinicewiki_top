## Infiltrating the Hideout: The Blizzard Gate and Drone Patrol

The final chapter begins amidst a freezing storm outside the syndicate's forward operating base. Sticks' hideout is concealed behind a heavy industrial tarp secured by frozen spikes. Your immediate priority is dismantling the perimeter barriers and uncovering the entrance keypad hidden under accumulated snowdrifts.

### Uncovering the Perimeter Clues

First, interact with the tarp and pull out the structural spikes to clear the main obstruction. Behind it lies an abandoned outpost buried under snow. Tap and sweep away the snowdrifts to reveal six discarded informational signs and a scattered pile of rubble.

Inspect the rubble to recover a bottle of high-proof vodka. In sub-zero temperatures, the ethanol acts as an impromptu de-icer. Apply the vodka directly to the frozen metal electrical box on the right side of the fence. This thaws the mechanism and exposes the numeric keypad along with an ominous bullet impact mark.

### Decoding the Perimeter Gate Lock

The keypad requires a 6-digit access code. The clue lies in cross-referencing the signs scattered around the clearing with the bullet hole visible on the lock housing:

1. Examine each uncovered sign carefully for bullet holes.
2. Group the signs by the quantity of bullet holes pierced through them (one, two, or three).
3. Extract the two-digit numbers printed on the corresponding signs in ascending order of hole count.

| Bullet Hole Count | Associated Sign Number | Keypad Contribution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1 Bullet Hole** | 99 | First two digits (`99`) |
| **2 Bullet Holes** | 12 | Middle two digits (`12`) |
| **3 Bullet Holes** | 24 | Final two digits (`24`) |

Entering **991224** disengages the electronic magnetic lock, swinging the gate open into the inner compound.

## Rescuing Murphy and the Fireplace Mantelpiece Puzzle

Blowing the entrance wall grants access to the upper level of the cabin, but disaster strikes immediately: the flooring collapses, leaving Murphy suspended over a lethal drop. 

### Stabilizing the Structural Bridge

Before Kate can explore deeper, she must construct an improvised crossing to pull Murphy to safety. Survey the immediate cabin interior to gather building materials:

* Pull back the decorative window curtain to discover a heavy **claw hammer**.
* Collect two long structural **support beams** leaning against the doorway frame.
* Use the hammer to pry loose two sturdy **wooden floor planks**.

Step back through the breached entrance to the exterior yard. Grab the fallen signpost from the ground, then use the hammer to pry two additional signs off the exterior wall frame. Note the etched geometric symbols exposed beneath the siding—these will prove crucial shortly.

Return inside the cabin. Lay the two support beams across the chasm, layer the five gathered planks across them, and position the metal spikes recovered from the outdoor tarp into the plank seams. Strike the spikes with your hammer to fasten the bridge securely. 

Cross the platform to haul Murphy up. In gratitude, Murphy hands over a distinct **gold ring** he salvaged from the precipice and points out an ornate mantle carving above the fireplace that clearly requires complementary components.

### Assembling and Rotating the Mantelpiece Rings

The fireplace mantelpiece functions as a hidden tumblers lock. To unlock the subterranean descent, you must track down two additional rings hidden in the cabin.

| Item | Location | Retrieval Tool Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gold Ring #1** | Retrieved by Murphy | Rescuing Murphy across bridge |
| **Gold Ring #2** | Buried in fireplace hearth ash | Direct interaction |
| **Gold Ring #3** | Suspended inside high skylight recess | Long Skylight Rod (found on mantel shelf) |

With all three rings in your inventory, tap the mantelpiece interface. Insert the rings into the concentric dial slots. 

To determine the correct dial orientation, recall the etched markings uncovered behind the exterior signs. Rotate each concentric ring until the etched runic markers align precisely with the casing indices. When positioned correctly, the central "V"-shaped alignment notch reveals a hidden sequence: **7427**. Entering this code opens the false back of the hearth, revealing an iron ladder descending into the syndicate's subterranean bunker.

## Sabotage in the Lower Bunker: Neutralizing Colt

Descending the iron ladder places Kate inside the nerve center of the ransomware gang. However, Colt—a heavily armed syndicate enforcer—stands guard over Dan’s kidnapped daughter, Caspia. Confronting Colt directly will trigger an alarm, necessitating a tactical sabotage approach.

### Gathering Bunker Tools and Distraction Assets

Ascend back up the ladder briefly to the cabin's upper floor to gather essential sabotage implements. Locate the spherical ornamental globe resting near the workstation:

1. Select your **hammer** and **chisel** from your inventory.
2. Crack open the reinforced globe casing to retrieve a bottle of high-viscosity **pancake syrup** and a **broken glass bottle**.
3. Descend back down to the lower bunker while Colt has his back turned toward the communications desk.

Locate the industrial backup generator humming in the corner of the room. Pour the thick syrup directly into the fuel intake valve. The sugary syrup rapidly gums the engine pistons, causing the generator to sputter, backfire, and belch smoke.

### Disabling Colt and Liberating Caspia

While Colt investigates the smoking generator, sneak up behind his station:

* Inspect Colt’s abandoned tactical backpack.
* Retrieve the high-voltage **Taser** and the printed **Close-Quarters Combat Manual**.
* Read the combat manual thoroughly to familiarize yourself with offensive stances, counter-blocks, and leverage strikes.
* Deploy the Taser directly against Colt to incapacitate him silently.
* Search Colt’s belt to obtain the **handcuff key**.

Interact with Caspia, who is chained to the structural column on the right side of the room. Use the key to unlock her restraints. Before you can escort her to safety, syndicate leader Sticks intercepts your escape path, forcing a direct standoff on the narrow subterranean bridge.

## The Final Confrontation: Defeating Sticks on the Bridge

The climax of **on thin ice chapter 4** hinges on an interactive three-round duel across a precarious footbridge. Sticks is armed, ruthless, and highly trained. You cannot brute-force this encounter; every action must exploit the tactical vulnerabilities detailed in the Combat Manual seized from Colt.

### Combat Mechanics Breakdown

The battle consists of three distinct phases. During each round, you must input a sequence of three combat commands (Strike, Block, or Dodge/Parry). Choosing an incorrect maneuver results in Sticks deflecting your attack and dealing critical damage.

| Encounter Phase | Sticks' Anticipated Action | Required Player Command Sequence | Tactical Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Round 1: Initial Rush** | Aggressive High Slash & Forward Lunge | **Block $\rightarrow$ Low Strike $\rightarrow$ Disarm** | Deflects opening blow, off-balances Sticks, chips guard |
| **Round 2: Defensive Recovery** | Mid-Stance Feint & Counter-Thrust | **Dodge $\rightarrow$ Counter Strike $\rightarrow$ Leg Sweep** | Evades feint, closes distance, forces Sticks to one knee |
| **Round 3: Desperation Surge** | Heavy Overhand Swing & Wild Grapple | **Parry $\rightarrow$ Body Blow $\rightarrow$ Finisher** | Shatters weapon grip, neutralizes Sticks, secures bridge |

Once the third round concludes successfully, Kate executes a decisive disarming maneuver, hurling Sticks’ weapon into the abyss and pinning her to the deck until backup arrives.

## Frequently Asked Questions About On Thin Ice Chapter 4

### What is the gate code in on thin ice chapter 4?
The code to open the perimeter gate in **on thin ice chapter 4** is **991224**. It is discovered by counting the bullet holes scattered across the signs buried under the snowdrifts (1 hole corresponds to 99, 2 holes to 12, and 3 holes to 24).

### How do you solve the mantelpiece ring puzzle in the cabin?
After retrieving all three golden rings (from Murphy, the fireplace ashes, and the high skylight using the rod), place them onto the mantelpiece. Rotate the dials until their etched symbols mirror the carving pattern discovered behind the outside wooden signs. When aligned, the "V" frame reveals the access passcode **7427**.

### How do you distract Colt without sounding the alarm?
You must crack open the globe on the upper floor using your hammer and chisel to retrieve pancake syrup. Pour the syrup into the lower bunker's backup generator. The engine failure forces Colt away from his station, allowing you to steal his taser and combat manual safely.

### What moves beat Sticks in the final chapter 4 fight?
Reference the tactical combat manual taken from Colt's backpack. To win the duel against Sticks across all three rounds, execute:
* **Round 1:** Block, Low Strike, Disarm.
* **Round 2:** Dodge, Counter Strike, Leg Sweep.
* **Round 3:** Parry, Body Blow, Finisher.
