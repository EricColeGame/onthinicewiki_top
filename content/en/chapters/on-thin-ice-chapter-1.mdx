## Police Station Terminal and Accessing the Cabin

Your investigation begins in the precinct where a cyber attack has compromised Kate's workstation behind yellow police tape. From this opening sequence, players must extract crucial intelligence and travel to informant Hacksaw's safe house.

### Step 1: Bypassing the Precinct PC Lock

2. Set the chip reader down onto the computer desk beside the terminal.
3. Observe the monitor screen as it prompts for a 5-digit hardware authentication key.
4. Examine the physical casing of the chip reader. Five digits stand out in heavy bold printing: **63849**.
5. Input **63849** on the desktop keyboard to bypass the ransomware barrier and unlock the case files.

## Investigating Hacksaw’s Cabin: Floorboards, Tattoos, and the Safe

Inside the dimly lit cabin, Kate and Murphy discover Hacksaw's body. The informant was murdered shortly before their arrival, meaning the killer could still be nearby.

### Gathering Cabin Items and Revealing the Floor Safe
1. Pull the rumpled mattress away from the wall and roll back the floor rug.
2. Push open the wooden window shutters to let natural daylight illuminate the crime scene.
3. Strip down the **cloth curtains** hanging beside the window.
4. Open the upper wall cabinets to discover an unfinished electrical puzzle mechanism and take the hand **hacksaw**.
5. Move toward the loose floorboards revealed beneath the rug. Use the hacksaw to cut through the seams and hoist out the heavy iron safe.

### Decoding the Tattoo Symbol Safe
The floor safe cannot be opened immediately because dried blood obscures the dial indicators. 

* Step outside to the front porch and dip the cloth curtains into the water puddle to create a **wet cloth**.
* Return inside and scrub away the bloodstains on the safe lid. A sequence of four icons is engraved above the tumbler: **Arrow, Skull, Heart, Wave**.
* Pick up the paper symbol strip resting beside Hacksaw's hand.
* Inspect Hacksaw’s body at four distinct points: his neck, arms, torso, and legs. Count every instance where these specific tattoo designs appear on his skin.

* Treat the puzzle like a classic sliding-tile grid. Utilize the outer perimeter overflow tiles to park non-essential numbered blocks while shifting the primary path blocks into place.
* Position your blocks in ascending numerical sequence starting from the near platform to the vehicle bank.
* Once the path locks securely into place, cross the ravine with your fire extinguisher ready.

## Extracting the Data: The Phone and the Triangulate Puzzle

Returning inside the cabin with your new tools allows you to breach Hacksaw's hidden compartments and charge his damaged communication device.

### Accessing the Dead Phone
1. Go back inside the cabin and plug the phone cord into the wall outlet next to the desk charger pad.
2. Examine the wheelchair parked in the corner. Apply your wrench to loosen the tight bolts on the wheel assembly, freeing a **small key**.
3. Walk back to the backyard and insert the small key into the cleaned storage box. Take the **screwdriver** and the second set of **triangles**.
4. Return to Hacksaw's body. Use the screwdriver to open the access panel on his prosthetic leg compartment to retrieve his personal **dead phone**.
5. Lay the phone down onto the plugged-in wireless charging pad. Wait through the dialogue between Kate and Murphy until the battery reaches functional capacity.

* Read through the unread SMS message threads. You will discover that Hacksaw was blackmailed by an extortionist and that an urgent courier delivery was deposited into his front mailbox.
* Step out to the front driveway and use the unlocked mobile phone to disengage the smart lock on the mailbox.

### Solving the Cabinet Triangulate Puzzle
Head back inside the cabin and zoom in on the unfinished puzzle mounted inside the wall cabinet. Place all gathered triangle tiles into the open matrix.

1. **Puzzle Objective:** Guide the electrical current from the starting node all the way to the bright yellow receiver triangle located on the far right edge.
2. **Rotation Rule:** Every single time the electrical pulse lands on a triangle tile, that specific tile automatically rotates 90 degrees clockwise.
3. **Execution Strategy:** Plan your route so that tiles rotate into positions that do not bounce the spark backward into dead ends. Focus on creating open directional pathways toward the right perimeter before committing your pulse.
4. **Resolution:** Completing the circuit opens the compartment mechanism to reveal a crucial **crime scene photo**.

### Revealing the Hard Drive
Approach the marked section of the cabin wallboard. Mount the **case letter**, the **cryptographic chart**, and the **crime scene photo** onto the board. 

Aligning the intersecting coordinates exposes a hidden wall safe containing Hacksaw’s master **hard drive**. Securing this drive ensures Kate and Murphy have the evidence needed to expose the conspiracy, bringing **on thin ice chapter 1** to an exciting close.
