## Infiltrating the Lobby: Bypassing and Fixing Lobby Bot

Your arrival at Sara Stikowski's corporate office immediately hits a snag when Lobby Bot bars your entry. You cannot reach the executive elevators until this robotic receptionist is dealt with.

### Step 1: Triggering the Sculpture Fall
When you step into the lobby, inspect the potted plant sitting against the back wall. Slide the plant aside to uncover a concealed wall switch. Flipping this switch destabilizes the modern suspended ceiling art, causing it to crash down onto the tile floor. Pick up the sharp **sculpture shard** from the debris.

### Step 2: Cracking the Symbol Lock Box
Approach Lobby Bot and cycle through all four available dialogue prompts. Three specific responses contain capitalized positional hints: **FIRST**, **CENTRAL**, and **LAST**. 

* Note which symbol corresponds to the starting clue (FIRST).
* Note which symbol corresponds to the middle clue (CENTRAL).
* Note which symbol matches the conclusion (LAST).

Approach the locked security box mounted in the lobby. Rotate the three tumblers to match the three symbols indicated by the bold dialogue cues. Opening the compartment yields a coil of **copper wire**.

### Step 3: Harvesting Components from the Atrium
Wrap the copper wire around Lobby Bot, causing an electrical surge that shorts out the machine. Slide the adjacent decorative plant aside to expose a hidden maintenance panel. Use the **sculpture shard** as a makeshift pry bar to wedge the door open. 

Inside the maintenance closet, retrieve the **ladder** and open the maintenance kit to take the **screwdriver**. Carry out the following actions:
1. Position the ladder directly beneath the overhead fluorescent fixture.
2. Reach into the overhead housing to salvage a **flash capacitor**.
3. Use the screwdriver on the wall junction box to unseat the **light switch**.

| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sculpture Shard** | Broken ceiling mobile | Wedges open the concealed maintenance hatch |
| **Copper Wire** | Three-symbol lobby lockbox | Wraps around Lobby Bot to cause an initial short |

### Step 4: The Lobby Bot Circuit Reprogramming
Attach the flash capacitor and light switch to the exposed wiring on Lobby Bot. Flip the switch. The resulting surge fractures the unit's internal processing board, dropping loose **computer chips**. Insert these chips into Lobby Bot's access panel to launch the chip alignment mini-game.

To complete the reprogramming puzzle, place every chip on the internal circuit grid so that adjacent connector paths match perfectly along every shared edge. Players should anchor corner chips first—identifiable by having only two active connection faces—and work inward toward the central nodes. Once every node connects without color or line mismatches, Lobby Bot reactivates in compliance mode, granting elevator access.

### Navigating the Interrogation
Approach Sticks at her desk. To lower her guard, convince her that Kate is a disgruntled civil servant seeking a payout rather than an officer serving a warrant.

Select the following dialogue chain in exact order:
1. **"We never get a reward."** (Hooks her belief that everyone has a price).
2. **"I have Hacksaw's evidence."** (Proves you possess leverage concerning the syndicate).
3. Place the **hard drive** onto her desk.
4. When prompted for your internal reaction, choose: **(think about dinner)**.

This nonchalant demeanor completely throws off Sticks' behavioral profiling. Realizing she has been outmaneuvered, she detonates an emergency smoke canister and flees into the facility.

| Dialogue Choice | Why It Works | Failure Consequence |

### Ventilating the Executive Office
Thick, blinding smoke blankets the office, making detailed investigation impossible. Drag the heavy desk chair out of the pathway toward the corner outlet. Pick up the **fan**, position it near the window corridor, and plug the power cord into the wall socket. The high-powered fan clears the room, revealing scattered clues across the floor and furniture.

Gather the following items from the cleared space:
* The discarded **hard drive** (Penny safeguards this for forensic analysis).
* A small **doll** resting near the side credenza.
* An extra **fan component** and decorative **bookcase ornaments**.
* A heavy braided **curtain rope** from the drape ties.

## Community Tips and Troubleshooting

Player experiences across community forums highlight a few common hang-ups during this chapter. If your game seems stalled, review these community-reported tips:

* **Unresponsive Hidden Panel**: In the reception lobby, players occasionally tap the hidden door without moving the second plant aside. Both the wall switch and the crawl hatch require shifting decorative greenery first.
* **Reprogramming Grid Dead-Ends**: If your circuit lines fail to connect, check your perimeter chips. Many players mistakenly place triple-connector chips on outer edges where one node is left hanging uselessly against the border wall.
* **Missing Clamp**: If you cannot find the clamp to retrieve the fallen plaque, reopen your inventory and tap the Polaroids again. The clamp is hidden physically behind the scattered photographs, not inside the safe wall cavity itself.
* **Broken Lamp Interaction**: You cannot pick up the desk lamp until you combine the duct tape with its frayed electrical cord while it is sitting on the office side-table.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the correct dialogue choices when talking to Sticks in on thin ice chapter 2?
To prevent Sara Stikowski from panicking prematurely in **on thin ice chapter 2**, choose the dialogue option "We never get a reward," followed by "I have Hacksaw's evidence." Place the hard drive on the table, and when the internal monologue prompt appears, select "(think about dinner)." This combination keeps her off-balance long enough to secure your position before she drops her smoke bomb.

### How do I find the combination for the lobby lockbox in on thin ice chapter 2?
Speak with Lobby Bot and ask all four available questions. Pay close attention to the text lines containing the capitalized words **FIRST**, **CENTRAL**, and **LAST**. Each word directly pairs with a distinctive shape. Enter those three shapes into the three-tumbler lockbox in that precise sequence to retrieve the copper wire.

### Where is the missing plaque located for the office bookshelf puzzle?
The brass plaque is lodged underneath the bottom ledge of the large bookshelf. You cannot reach it with your bare hands. Open your inventory, inspect the Polaroid photos recovered from the safe, and collect the small mechanical clamp hidden between the photo frames. Use the clamp on the gap under the bookshelf to retrieve the plaque.

### Why won't the smoke fan turn on in the executive suite?
The fan requires two setup steps before it will operate. First, drag the large office chair out of the way to uncover the electrical wall outlet. Second, pick up the fan, set it on the floor near the doorway corridor, and plug the power cord directly into the newly exposed wall socket. Once powered, the fan will automatically clear the smoke screen.
