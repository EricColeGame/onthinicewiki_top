## Step-by-Step Solutions for Tough Early Puzzles

When progressing through on thin ice ae mysteries, several multi-stage codes require meticulous attention to detail. Skipping steps or guessing codes rarely works, as clues are embedded directly into character bodies, machine serials, and environmental fixtures.

### 1. Precinct Computer & Cabin Keypad Access

At Kate's desk, plug the chip reader into the computer terminal. By examining the serial digits stamped across the reader casing, you will locate five bolded numbers: **63849**. Entering these digits bypasses the ransomware lock screen on Kate's workstation.

Upon arriving at the snowbound cabin:
- Collect the replacement wheel, nozzle, and fuel canister scattered across the driveway.
- Assemble the parts onto the dead snowblower and clear the snow banks.
- Pick up the loose metal number tiles and arrange them on the signpost.
- Shift the numbers along the top rail to reveal the combination **40827**.
- Key in **40827** on the electronic cabin door lock to enter the interior.

### 2. Cracking the Informant's Blood-Stained Safe

Inside the cabin, pull back the mattress and rug to discover cut floorboards. Cut through the boards using the hacksaw located in the wardrobe. Pull out the locked safe, take the curtain fabric, soak it in the melted snow outside, and wipe away the dried blood.

To find the safe passcode, examine the symbol strip clutched in Hacksaw’s hand and compare it against the tattoos inked across his body:

| Symbol Clue | Body Tattoo Count | Resulting Digit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Arrow** | Counted across arms and torso | **9** |
| **Skull** | Counted across shoulders and chest | **4** |
| **Heart** | Single tattoo located on the shoulder | **1** |
| **Wave** | Counted across forearm bands | **6** |

Entering **9416** pops the safe latch, granting you the lockpin gun, three geometric triangles, and an urgent warning letter.

```
Code Calculation Breakdown:
[Arrow: 9] + [Skull: 4] + [Heart: 1] + [Wave: 6] = Passcode 9416
```

### 3. The Ice Stairway Sliding Block Challenge

Unlocking the rear exit reveals a burning vehicle crash. Before reaching the flames, you must construct a stable platform over a slippery gap. Tap the frozen ice blocks to trigger the sliding block puzzle.

| Movement Step | Target Block | Directional Shift | Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1** | Top row block #1 | Slide Right into empty track | Opens vertical corridor |
| **Phase 2** | Central block #3 | Move Downward to base slot | Establishes lower support |
| **Phase 3** | Numbered block #2 | Slide Left, then Down | Locks step 2 in ascending order |
| **Phase 4** | Numbered block #4 | Slide Up, Right, and Drop | Completes sequential stair bridge |

Once the bridge is stable, douse the vehicle fire with your extinguisher. Search the occupants for a mechanic's wrench and a steel crowbar, pry open the vehicle door, and extract the mobile charging cable.

### 4. Triangulate Circuit and Secret Wall Safe

Back inside, use the wrench on the broken wheelchair to retrieve a small key, unlock the outdoor toolbox for a screwdriver, and open Hacksaw's prosthetic leg to retrieve his dead smartphone.

After charging the phone, unlock the facial recognition lock by pointing the lens at Hacksaw's face. Read the SMS inbox to discover that a courier dropped off key documents inside the front mailbox. Unlock the mailbox using the phone's wireless link to acquire the final geometric triangles and an architectural chart.

Place all collected triangles into the bedroom cabinet puzzle board. To solve the Triangulate puzzle:
- Guide the pulsing electrical dot toward the yellow terminal on the far right.
- Every time the electrical charge enters a triangle, that node rotates 90 degrees clockwise.
- Trace routes that utilize empty apexes so the next pulse aligns with the exit wire.
- Completing the circuit unlocks a hidden drawer containing an archival photograph.

Hang the letter, structural chart, and photograph on the evidence board to trigger the mechanical wall safe, uncovering Hacksaw’s uncorrupted external hard drive.

## Advanced Strategy and Resource Management Tips

Veteran players of on thin ice ae mysteries recommend taking screenshots of murals and message logs rather than burning hint stars. Puzzle hint stars are rare resources in the game; saving them for later, more intricate chapters ensures you will not be blocked when puzzle complexity ramps up.

Based on general player experience across community walkthrough forums, here are practical rules of thumb for staying efficient:

- **Check Every Compartment Twice:** Containers frequently hold secondary items beneath a top layer. Always tap an open compartment a second time to ensure Kate grabs all available evidence.
- **Inspect Prosthetics and Clothing:** Body examinations require inspecting multiple distinct zones (hands, pockets, limbs, and neck). Missing a single zone halts safe and door unlocks.
- **Track Symbol Alignments:** If a code requires matching symbols, draw out the icons on paper. Tattoos in dim light can blur wave lines and skull emblems together.
- **Rotate Inventory Items:** Certain clues must be examined closely inside the inventory screen before they register as usable puzzle keys in the room environment.

Mastering the pacing of on thin ice ae mysteries will save you stars, streamline your deduction speed, and preserve the cinematic thrill of Kate Gray's high-stakes race against the clock.

## Frequently Asked Questions About On Thin Ice AE Mysteries

### How long does it take to beat on thin ice ae mysteries?
Most players complete the entire story in approximately 3 to 5 hours, depending on how quickly they solve spatial puzzles like the Ice Stairway and whether they use hints. Chapter 1 generally takes between 30 to 45 minutes for first-time players.

### Can you skip puzzles in on thin ice ae mysteries?
Yes. If you find a particular puzzle too difficult or frustrating, you can bypass the minigame by spending in-game stars. However, stars are limited unless purchased, so it is recommended to use written solutions rather than skipping puzzles entirely.

### What should I do if the cabin phone will not unlock with Hacksaw's face?
Ensure you have charged the phone completely using the wireless dock and car cable first. Kate and Murphy must finish their dialogue sequence before the phone can be held up to the deceased informant for biometric recognition.

### Why won't the floor safe accept the 9416 code?
You must first wash the dried blood off the keypad using the wet curtain cloth. If the blood remains on the mechanism, the keypad remains unclickable, preventing the combination from registering.
