## Chapter 1 Walkthrough: From Precinct Lockdown to Hacksaw's Cabin

The opening chapter acts as an intense tutorial for the environmental deduction systems that define the entire adventure. Below is a structured breakdown of the steps necessary to navigate Chapter 1 successfully.

```
Precinct Terminal -> Hacksaw's Perimeter -> Cabin Interior -> Burnt Car Exterior -> Triangulation Safe
```

### 1. The Ransomware Terminal Breach
Upon arriving at Kate's taped-off precinct desk, normal system access is blocked by an active ransomware lock.

*   Locate the standalone **chip reader** inside the evidence box adjacent to the desk.
*   Place the chip reader directly onto Kate's work station next to the monitor.
*   Observe the serial plate on the chip reader. Identify the bolded numeric sequence: **63849**.
*   Input **63849** into the terminal console to clear the station lockout and retrieve the operational dispatch report.

### 2. Clearing the Snowdrift at Hacksaw's Cabin
Kate and Murphy arrive at the remote cabin of their informant, Hacksaw, following reports of a violent firefight. A thick wall of snow blocks entry.

*   Scour the yard perimeter to retrieve three mechanical parts: the **wheel**, the **nozzle**, and the **fuel canister**.
*   Combine these three items onto the broken snowblower sitting in the yard.
*   Activate the restored snowblower to clear the snowbank, uncovering scattered **metal numbers**.
*   Examine the number board to the right of the porch. Arrange the loose metal digits into the top formation to reveal the sequence: **40827**.
*   Approach the cabin entrance keypad and submit **40827** to open the reinforced door.

## Forensics and Safe Cracking: Decentering Hacksaw's Secret

Once inside the ransacked cabin, the atmosphere darkens significantly. Hacksaw has been killed in the shootout, leaving his body and personal effects as your sole investigative assets.

### Uncovering the Floor Safe
To secure the cabin's interior evidence:

1.  Shift the floor mattress and rug to locate loose floorboards.
2.  Open the window to restore natural light, revealing the hanging **cloth curtains**. Tear them down for utility use.
3.  Inspect the kitchen cabinetry to secure a handheld **hacksaw**.
4.  Use the hacksaw on the compromised floorboards to extract a heavy metal safe caked in dried blood.
5.  Exit back into the yard, moisten the cloth curtains in a melted snow puddle, and return to wipe the blood clear from the safe's numeric lock.

### The Tattoo Cipher Solution
Around the cabin interior, you will find a narrow strip printed with four distinct glyphs: an arrow, a skull, a heart, and an ocean wave. Inspecting Hacksaw's body reveals numerous criminal and military tattoos scattered across his arms, neck, and torso.

| Glyph Symbol | Body Tattoo Locations | Total Count (Digit) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Arrow** | Forearms, collarbone, upper chest, knuckles | **9** |
| **Skull** | Biceps, shoulder blade, wrist | **4** |
| **Heart** | Inner forearm | **1** |
| **Wave** | Ribcage, lower abdomen, neck | **6** |

Submitting the code **9416** into the safe opens the vault, yielding the **lockpin gun**, a crucial **handwritten letter**, and decorative **triangles** required for a late-stage puzzle.

```
Symbol Strip Order: Arrow -> Skull -> Heart -> Wave
```

## Resolving Complex Logic: The Ice Stairway and Triangulation Puzzles

Beyond static observation, the **on thin ice game** incorporates dynamic spatial puzzles that challenge your planning and routing abilities. These challenges appear back-to-back toward the conclusion of Chapter 1.

### The Ice Stairway Slide Puzzle
Using the lockpin gun on the cabin's rear exit exposes a courtyard featuring a burning getaway car. Before you can address the fire, you must reach the upper terrace where fire safety equipment is stored.

Interacting with the frozen terrace triggers the **Ice Stairway** mini-game. You must slide numbered ice blocks across slick terrain to dock them into corresponding numeric slots.

*   **Mechanical Rule**: Blocks do not stop until they collide with an obstacle, wall, or another block.
*   **Buffer Blocks**: Utilize the unnumbered auxiliary blocks as artificial bumpers to arrest the momentum of numbered blocks.
*   **Order of Operations**: Anchor the highest numbers first (bottom slots) to prevent obstructing paths needed for lower numbers.

Once completed, you obtain the **fire extinguisher** and **cleaning oil** from the grill area, enabling you to extinguish the vehicle and claim a **wrench** and **crowbar** from the fallen gunmen.

| Inventory Component | Source Location | Application Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cleaning Oil** | Under patio grill tarp | Rusted outdoor lockbox (clears corrosion) |
| **Fire Extinguisher** | Upper terrace storage | Blazing vehicle in back yard |
| **Crowbar** | Armed assailant's remains | Jammed car door (yields charging cable) |
| **Wrench** | Assailant near fence | Broken wheelchair wheel (drops lockbox key) |
| **Small Key** | Wheelchair rim assembly | De-rusted yard lockbox (yields screwdriver) |
| **Screwdriver** | Unlocked yard box | Hidden prosthetic compartment on Hacksaw's leg |

### Hacksaw’s Phone and the Triangulate Puzzle
With the screwdriver in hand, pry open the secret compartment built into Hacksaw's prosthetic leg to retrieve his dead smartphone.

1.  Connect the car's charging cable to the cabin's wireless charging mat.
2.  Power on the phone and align the screen with Hacksaw's face to bypass facial recognition locks.
3.  Read through the threatening message logs, noting mentions of an external mailbox delivery.
4.  Unlock the front mailbox using the phone's verification signal to collect the remaining **triangles** and a **topological chart**.
5.  Return to the cabinet interior and insert all triangle pieces to trigger the **Triangulate Puzzle**.

In the Triangulate puzzle, your objective is to steer an electric pulse from the left starting node to the yellow target triangle on the far right. Every time an electric spark enters a triangle, that tile **rotates 90 degrees clockwise**.

```
[Start Pulse] -> Triangle Node A (Rotates 90°)
              -> Triangle Node B (Rotates 90°)
              -> Triangle Node C (Rotates 90°) -> [Yellow Goal Node]
```

To solve this efficiently, trace your route backwards from the yellow endpoint. Identify the orientation each triangle must rest in when the pulse hits it, compensating for the mandatory quarter-turn upon entry. Solving the grid unlocks an evidentiary photograph. Combine the photograph, the chart, and the letter on the study wall to unseal Hacksaw's encrypted hard drive, successfully wrapping up Chapter 1.

## Essential Detective Strategies for On Thin Ice

Players encountering the **on thin ice game** for the first time often stumble over basic inventory oversights or misread clues. Applying disciplined investigative habits will keep you from squandering stars on automated puzzle skips.

### 1. Sweep Every Scene in Three Passes
Whenever Kate enters a new environment, conduct your search in three dedicated sweeps:
*   **Loose Items Sweep**: Tap obvious floor debris, open containers, and untied tarps.
*   **Fastener Sweep**: Look for physical fasteners—screws, rusted padlocks, pryable boards—that match common escape tools (screwdrivers, wrenches, crowbars).
*   **Text and Symbol Sweep**: Write down any numbers, unusual patterns, or stylized graffiti immediately. In the **on thin ice game**, numbers found in the background are almost never set dressing; they invariably tie directly to an upcoming padlock or keypad.

### 2. Treat Spatial Puzzles Like Chess Problems
Sliding-block sequences, such as the Ice Stairway, penalize aimless clicking. When an ice block shoots across the screen, it often resets the alignment of another track.
*   Map the final position of each block mentally before moving a single tile.
*   Identify which tiles act as natural anchors (corners, edges).
*   Never burn an auxiliary block into a corner where it cannot be extracted; keep floating obstacles in central lanes where they can redirect other pieces.

## Frequently Asked Questions About On Thin Ice

### What is the safe code in Chapter 1 of the On Thin Ice game?
The safe combination inside Hacksaw's cabin is **9416**. You deduce this code by cross-referencing the paper symbol strip (Arrow, Skull, Heart, Wave) with the corresponding tattoos marked across Hacksaw's body. Counting each matching glyph yields 9 arrows, 4 skulls, 1 heart, and 6 waves.

### Where do you find all the triangle pieces for the cabinet puzzle?
The triangle pieces are split across two separate discovery points in Chapter 1. You receive the first set from inside the bloodstained floor safe after entering code **9416**. The final set is locked inside Hacksaw's front yard mailbox, which can only be unlocked after powering his smartphone and scanning the mailbox's digital receiver.

### Is the On Thin Ice game playable as a standalone mystery?
Yes. While the narrative references Kate Gray's previous traumatic encounter in Echo Bay and advances her personal relationship with Detective Murphy, the case itself is entirely self-contained. Newcomers can jump directly into the **on thin ice game** without prior knowledge of earlier Adventure Escape titles and follow the syndicate investigation without confusion.
