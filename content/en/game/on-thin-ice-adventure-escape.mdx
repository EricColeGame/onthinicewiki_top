## Complete Chapter 1 Walkthrough: From Ransomware to Crime Scene

Chapter 1 throws players immediately into a crime scene wrapped inside another crime scene. Below is the full step-by-step breakdown to navigate the precinct lockdown, access the remote mountain hideout, and retrieve the vital hard drive.

### Phase 1: Breaching the Compromised Precinct

The investigation begins inside the police station, where Kate's personal workstation is roped off with caution tape following a terminal breach.

1. **Locate the Peripheral**: Inspect the supply box near the taped perimeter and retrieve the chip reader.
2. **Access the Computer Terminal**: Place the chip reader on Kate's workstation desk. Observe the hardware unit carefully: five highlighted, bold numeric characters reveal the passcode: **63849**.
3. **Log into the System**: Input **63849** into the computer monitor to bypass the security wall and pinpoint Murphy's field rendezvous with his informant, known by the alias "Hacksaw."

### Phase 2: Clearing the Snowbound Cabin Entrance

Arriving at Hacksaw's coordinates amidst an intensifying blizzard, Kate and Murphy find bullet impacts and a silent telephone line. Heavy snowdrifts block the cabin exterior.

1. **Reassemble the Snowblower**: Search the perimeter to collect three essential maintenance items: a wheel, an output nozzle, and a canister of motor fuel.
2. **Clear the Pathway**: Attach all three items to the disabled snowblower, start the engine, and clear the massive snow accumulation obscuring the entryway.
3. **Assemble the Exterior Keypad Code**: Collect the stray metal number plates scattered under the cleared snow. Fit them onto the wooden pegboard beside the door. Arrange the plates across the upper tier to form the access sequence: **40827**.
4. **Enter the Cabin**: Punch **40827** into the digital door lock to enter the murder scene.

Inside the cabin, Hacksaw lies motionless on a cot, surrounded by signs of a deadly gunfight. To gather clues, players must thoroughly examine the room.

1. **Prepare the Space**: Slide the floor mattress and area rug aside. Open the wooden window shutters to let natural light fill the dark cabin interior. Strip the hanging cloth curtains from the rod.
2. **Retrieve the Cutting Tool**: Open the wall cabinets to locate a miniature hacksaw tool, noting the locked triangulation panel nearby.
3. **Analyze the Victim's Body**: Inspect Hacksaw closely. Note that his mobile device casing is completely empty. Notice the mechanical seam denoting a prosthetic compartment along his lower leg, and collect the symbol parchment strip resting beside his fingers.
4. **Extract the Safe**: Use the hacksaw to cut through the unsecured floorboards beneath the cot and pull out the heavy iron strongbox.
5. **Clean the Dial**: Walk outside to the exterior puddle to soak the cloth curtain. Return indoors and scrub away the dried blood covering the dial panel.
6. **Decipher the Tattoo Combination**: Match the symbols listed on your parchment strip with the matching tattoos inked across Hacksaw's skin:

Using the lockpin gun on the secured back door reveals an outside courtyard dominated by a blazing getaway vehicle and fallen cartel mercenaries.

1. **Douse the Flames**: Remove the protective tarp from the outdoor barbecue grill to claim a fire extinguisher and a can of industrial cleaning oil. Apply the fire extinguisher directly to the vehicle inferno.
2. **Treat the Rusted Chest**: Brush away the snow near the porch to expose an oxidized lockbox. Apply the cleaning oil to dissolve the surface rust.
3. **Solve the Ice Stairway Puzzle**: Tap the frozen step blocks leading to the lower terrace. Slide the numbered blocks along the tracks until each block rests in its corresponding numerical slot.
4. **Loot the Fallen Gunmen**: Search the eliminated targets by the car to secure an iron crowbar and an adjustable wrench.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do if the safe combination 9416 doesn't unlock the floor safe?
In **on thin ice adventure escape**, the safe mechanism will not accept input until you have wiped the blood off the lock face with the wet cloth and inspected the symbol parchment strip. Ensure you have retrieved the cloth from the window, dampened it in the exterior water puddle, cleaned the safe, and viewed Hacksaw's tattoos in detail.

### How do I bypass Hacksaw’s facial recognition lock on his smartphone?
After connecting the phone cable to the cabin outlet and letting the battery charge, pick up the smartphone and tap Hacksaw's face directly. Because the deceased informant's biometric data is stored locally on the device, holding the camera up to him satisfies the security check and opens his text log.

### Can I skip puzzles in On Thin Ice Adventure Escape if I get stuck?

However, practicing systematic route planning on puzzles like the Ice Stairway and Triangulate board usually allows you to solve them without spending currency.

### How many chapters are in On Thin Ice Adventure Escape?
