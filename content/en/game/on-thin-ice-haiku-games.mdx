## Step-by-Step Chapter 1 Puzzle Breakdown and Solutions

The opening chapter throws players into high alert immediately following the ransomware breach. From Kate's quarantined workstation to the snow-covered cabin where informant Hacksaw met his fate, navigating the early scenes of **On Thin Ice Haiku Games** can test even experienced puzzle solvers.

### 1. Precinct Breach and the Station Computer
Upon discovering Kate's desk cordoned off with crime scene tape, locate the hardware accessories needed to bypass the lock screen:

* Retrieve the **chip reader** tucked inside the desktop storage box.
* Connect the chip reader to Kate's workstation terminal.
* Inspect the exterior casing of the reader to spot the five emboldened digits: **63849**.
* Type **63849** into the computer interface to decrypt the system and unlock Murphy's lead.

### 2. Clearing the Snow and Unlocking Hacksaw’s Cabin
Arriving at the snowbound cabin hideout, Kate and Murphy discover signs of a deadly gunfight. To clear the drifted snow blocking access to the entrance:

* Gather the three missing snowblower components: the **wheel**, the **exhaust nozzle**, and the **fuel canister**.
* Install the components onto the snowblower unit in the yard and activate the machine.
* Clear away the snowdrifts to uncover a scatter of metallic numbering tiles.
* Place the metal number tiles onto the exterior display board. Arrange them along the upper bracket to assemble the combination: **40827**.
* Input **40827** on the electronic keypad beside the cabin doorway to gain access.

### 3. The Informant’s Tattoos and the Hidden Safe
Inside the cabin, clear the floor by pulling back the mattress and rug, then pull down the cloth curtains to illuminate the crime scene. Examine the informant's body, his empty mobile phone case, and the patterned strip resting by his hand.

* Collect the **hacksaw** from the interior cabinetry.
* Cut through the loose floorboards using the hacksaw to expose a bloodstained steel safe.
* Step outside to dampen the cloth curtains in the melted puddle, then return indoors to wipe the safe clean.
* Examine Hacksaw's body tattoos and tally the occurrences of the symbols shown on the paper strip:
  * **Arrows:** 9
  * **Skulls:** 4
  * **Hearts:** 1
  * **Waves:** 6
* Enter **9416** into the safe dial to retrieve the **lockpin gun**, a written letter, and the first set of acrylic triangles.

## Mastering the Core Minigames and Mechanics in On Thin Ice

Solving complex logic grids in **On Thin Ice Haiku Games** demands methodical thinking rather than random trial and error. The adventure introduces several distinct puzzle formats that reappear with increasing complexity throughout subsequent chapters.

### Ice Block Sliding Physics
The ice block puzzles incorporate strict sliding constraints where blocks glide until hitting an obstacle or barrier. Community reports frequently note players getting stuck when moving perimeter blocks prematurely.

* Always map the stopping points before executing a move.
* Identify stationary immovable rocks or posts to serve as brakes for high-value numbered blocks.
* Utilize the auxiliary buffer slots to temporarily store non-essential blocks while clearing the primary lane.

### The Triangulate Energy Grid
The cabinet circuit puzzle challenges players to direct an electrical impulse from a start node to an endpoint across rotating triangular tiles. Every time the electrical charge transitions onto a triangle, that specific tile rotates 90 degrees clockwise.

Planning two steps ahead is crucial. If your outgoing vector relies on a tile face that shifts upon entry, your path will divert into dead ends. Trace backwards from the destination node to determine which rotation states are viable upon final entry.

## Frequently Asked Questions About On Thin Ice Haiku Games

### What is the safe code in Chapter 1 of On Thin Ice Haiku Games?
The safe combination beneath the cabin floorboards in **On Thin Ice Haiku Games** is **9416**. To derive this code, examine Hacksaw’s body and tally the total count of the symbols displayed on the paper strip found near his hand: 9 Arrows, 4 Skulls, 1 Heart, and 6 Waves.

### How do I unlock the computer at the police station?
To unlock Kate’s workstation in Chapter 1, place the chip reader found in the nearby storage box onto her desk. Inspect the bold numbers printed across the reader's hardware label: **63849**. Enter **63849** on the workstation monitor keypad to bypass the ransomware screen.

### How does the Triangulate puzzle work in the cabin cabinet?
The Triangulate puzzle requires you to guide an electrical current from the left side to the yellow target triangle on the far right. Each time the current moves onto a triangle, that tile rotates clockwise. You must map a trajectory that accounts for these automatic rotations so that exit pathways remain open as the current advances.

### Where is the phone cord located to charge Hacksaw's phone?
The charging cable is located inside the charred vehicle behind the cabin. Solve the Ice Stairway slider puzzle to reach the fire, extinguish the flames with the fire extinguisher, and use the crowbar obtained from one of the victims to pry open the vehicle door. The cord is resting on the seat inside.
