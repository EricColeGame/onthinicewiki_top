## Step-by-Step Chapter 1 Walkthrough: The Precinct and Hacksaw's Cabin

Chapter 1 establishes the grim atmosphere and high-stakes tension that define the overarching narrative. When a sudden ransomware breach strikes the police department, Kate discovers her workstation sealed behind crime scene tape. From there, the investigation moves toward a tense rendezvous with an informant known as Hacksaw.

### 1. Breaching the Precinct Security

Your first objective requires bypassing the ransomware lockout at Kate's desk:
* Collect the **chip reader** resting inside the nearby box.
* Place the chip reader directly onto the desk interface to prompt the monitor display.
* Inspect the peripheral device carefully to spot the bolded numerical sequence: **63849**.
* Enter **63849** into the workstation computer to bypass the terminal lock and retrieve your operational briefing.

### 2. Clearing the Blizzard at the Safehouse

Arriving at the secluded cabin, Kate and Murphy discover that a firefight has occurred, leaving Hacksaw unreachable. Before accessing the cabin interior, you must clear the heavy snowdrifts blocking the perimeter:
* Collect the missing **wheel**, **blower nozzle**, and **canister of fuel** scattered around the yard.
* Install these components onto the stalled snowblower to restore engine power.
* Clear the snow banks to reveal discarded **metal numbers** near the porch.
* Mount the numbers onto the outdoor signboard, arranging them along the upper bar to calculate the access sequence: **40827**.
* Input **40827** on the electronic cabin keypad to unlock the front door.

### 3. Autopsy Clues and the Cabin Safe

Inside the safehouse, the atmosphere shifts into a forensic room-escape challenge. A deceased victim rests on the floor, requiring thorough examination to uncover buried intel.

```
Safe Code Deduction Process:
1. Examine body tattoos -> Note symbols: Arrow, Skull, Heart, Wave
2. Clean safe face with damp cloth -> Expose symbol lock order
3. Tally counts across body:
   - Arrows: 9
   - Skulls: 4
   - Hearts: 1
   - Waves:  6
Result Code = [ 9 - 4 - 1 - 6 ]
```

* Push aside the area rug and mattress, then open the cabin window to illuminate the room.
* Retrieve the **cloth curtains** and collect the **hacksaw** stored inside the overhead cupboards.
* Inspect the informant's body across four distinct points, noting his prosthetic leg compartment and the symbol strip near his hand.
* Use the hacksaw to sever the weakened floorboards beneath the rug and hoist out the heavy iron safe.
* Head outside to dampen the cloth curtain in an open puddle, then return inside to wipe the dried blood from the safe dial.
* Count the corresponding symbol tattoos displayed across the victim's skin: 9 Arrows, 4 Skulls, 1 Heart, and 6 Waves.
* Punch in the combination **9416** to open the safe, retrieving the **lockpin gun**, decorative **triangles**, and an urgent letter.

### The Ice Stairway Sliding Puzzle

Outside the cabin's rear exit, a burning vehicle blocks access to crucial evidence. To cross the freezing embankment, you must solve the Ice Stairway puzzle by sliding numbered ice blocks into corresponding slots.

1. **Assess the Free Slots:** The puzzle grants extra resting tiles along the perimeter. Do not rush to push numbered blocks straight into place.
2. **Move Lower Values First:** Park higher-numbered blocks in peripheral relief zones while you align the base foundation.
3. **Prevent Dead-Ends:** Avoid pushing two sequential numbers against a corner wall before their destination slot is cleared, as blocks cannot jump over adjacent obstructions.

### The Cabinet Triangulate Circuit

Once you recover all matching triangle pieces from the safe, the utility box, and the porch mailbox, you can access the electrical mechanism inside the kitchen cabinet.

* **Objective:** Direct an electrical pulse from the left circuit source to the yellow triangular terminal on the far right edge.
* **The Catch:** Every time your electrical current passes through a triangular node, that node rotates clockwise by 60 degrees.
* **Strategy:** Plan two steps ahead. If a pathway looks open but leaves a subsequent node facing a dead wall, rotate an alternate loop first to prepare the downstream connection before triggering the primary line.
* Solving this mechanism yields a crucial **photo evidence slip**, which works in tandem with the shipping chart and letter to open the final wall safe containing Hacksaw's encrypted hard drive.

## Actionable Tips to Elevate Your Investigation

Succeeding across late-game scenarios requires disciplined investigative habits. Many players find themselves stuck simply because they overlooked a subtle screen detail or misplaced an inventory asset.

```
                    Investigator Checklist
 [ ] Clear snow & obstructions before inspecting distant doors
 [ ] Double-check collected documents for subtle numerical hints
 [ ] Clean dirty surfaces with environmental water sources
 [ ] Test unlocked biometric devices on room characters
 [ ] Re-examine repaired tools for secondary mechanical uses
```

* **Inspect Object Descriptions Thoroughly:** Whenever Kate picks up an item, tap it in your inventory bag. Text prompts often reveal whether an item is rusty, incomplete, or requires cleaning before use.
* **Scan the Periphery for Interactables:** If a puzzle seems impossible due to missing parts, pan your eyes across the extreme borders of the screen. Items like the wheel nozzle or exterior rust oil are frequently tucked along bottom screen edges.
* **Leverage Environmental Clues for Passcodes:** Passwords in the game rarely appear as plain text written on walls. Look for bold fonts on electronic peripherals, math signs on signposts, or repeating tattoos.
* **Consult Official Game Resources When Stuck:** For news, updates, and community event schedules, keep an eye on the [official Haiku Games portal](https://www.haikugames.com), where the development team shares announcements for new mystery releases and chapter balances.
* **Preserve In-Game Stars:** Save your hint currency exclusively for sliding and circuit-based mini-games. Numerical ciphers can almost always be deduced by writing down observations on a scrap pad.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What genre does On Thin Ice gameplay fall into?
The title combines point-and-click adventure mechanics, escape room logic puzzles, and forensic detective investigations. Players explore static scenes, manage an active inventory, and solve mechanical mini-games to progress the narrative.

### How long does it take to complete the entire On Thin Ice adventure?
Based on player experience and average puzzle-solving speeds, a full playthrough typically spans between 3 to 5 hours across all chapters. Experienced escape room players who rarely consult hints may finish it closer to the 2.5-hour mark.

### Can you bypass difficult mini-games if you get stuck during On Thin Ice gameplay?
Yes. The game provides a skip option for complex mini-games such as the Ice Stairway or Triangulate circuit puzzles. Bypassing these challenges requires spending in-game stars, which can be gathered by tapping hidden stars hidden across game backgrounds or purchased via optional in-app options.

### What is the cabin safe code in Chapter 1 of the game?
The safe combination inside Hacksaw's cabin is **9416**. It is calculated by cleaning the safe dial with a damp curtain and counting the corresponding tattoo symbols located on the victim's body: 9 arrows, 4 skulls, 1 heart, and 6 waves.
