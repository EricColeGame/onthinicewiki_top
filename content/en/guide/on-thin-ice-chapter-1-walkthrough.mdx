## Police Station: Bypassing the Ransomware Lockout

The chapter opens amidst chaos inside the precinct. Kate Gray's desk is cordoned off with yellow crime scene tape due to an active ransomware outbreak. 

To clear this preliminary roadblock in your **on thin ice chapter 1 walkthrough** run:

1. Tap on the storage crate located to the lower left of Kate’s desk to acquire the **Chip Reader**.
2. Place the chip reader on the workstation desk next to the computer screen.
3. Observe the monitor, which prompts you for a 5-digit authorization override code.
4. Inspect the chip reader in your inventory closely. Five numbers stand out printed in bold relief: **63849**.
5. Input **63849** into the computer console. 

Overriding the terminal reveals that Murphy’s informant, an elusive figure known as Hacksaw, has missed contact following a firefight at his remote cabin. Murphy and Kate immediately depart into a mounting snowstorm.

## Cabin Exterior: Clearing Snow and Gaining Entry

Upon arriving at Hacksaw’s mountain cabin, Murphy discovers bullet holes and an unanswered phone. A severe blizzard has buried the walkway in heavy snowdrifts, blocking entry.

### Snowblower Assembly and Operation

To clear a path to the front porch, you need to repair the abandoned snowblower sitting near the driveway:

* Locate the **Wheel** lying near the fence post on the left.
* Pick up the **Nozzle** partially submerged near the porch steps.
* Retrieve the **Fuel Canister** resting by the generator shelter.
* Combine all three components with the broken snowblower.

Tap the repaired machine to blow away the snow banks. Clearing the snow exposes a scattering of loose **Metal Numbers** along the frozen soil.

### The Metal Number Plaque Puzzle

Pick up the metal digits and examine the wooden board mounted directly to the right of the front door. You must position the numbers along the top row according to the mathematical clues printed on the lower rows. 

```
Board Clue Layout:
Top Slots:  [ 4 ] [ 0 ] [ 8 ] [ 2 ] [ 7 ]
Logic: Matching sum alignments indicated on lower wooden slats.
```

Arranging the tiles reveals the numerical sequence **40827**. Tap on the electronic keypad lock on the cabin door, punch in **40827**, and head inside.

## Cabin Interior: Hacksaw’s Body and the Safe

Inside, you find Hacksaw deceased on the floor, surrounded by disarray. To thoroughly search the room, take several investigative steps before touching the body:

* Drag the overturned mattress aside to clear the floor.
* Pull back the area rug to expose loose timber floorboards.
* Open the window shutters to illuminate the dark room.
* Strip down the hanging **Cloth Curtains**.
* Open the overhead kitchen cabinets to grab the **Hacksaw** and view an incomplete circuit board.

### Forensic Examination of Hacksaw

Examine the victim's body closely. You will notice four points of interest:

1. An empty phone case in his chest pocket.
2. A concealed mechanical seam along his prosthetic leg.
3. Multiple distinct tattoo groupings across his skin.
4. A small **Symbol Strip** clenched near his right hand.

```
Symbol Strip Order:
[ Arrow ] -> [ Skull ] -> [ Heart ] -> [ Wave ]
```

### Floorboard Safe & Tattoo Decryption

Use the hacksaw from the cabinet to cut through the loose floorboards under the rug, lifting out a heavy steel safe. The safe's keypad is smeared with dried blood, obscuring the interface.

Step back outside to the front porch and submerge your cloth curtains into the slushy puddle to create a **Wet Cloth**. Re-enter the cabin and clean the dial. 

To deduce the 4-digit code, count the total occurrences of each icon from the symbol strip found among Hacksaw's body tattoos.

### Tattoo Symbol Breakdown

| Symbol Type | Body Locations | Total Count | Safe Position |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Arrow** | Left shoulder (3), Right forearm (4), Neck (2) | **9** | 1st Digit |

Enter **9416** into the clean safe lock. Inside, collect the **Letter**, a set of decorative **Triangles**, and a **Lockpin Gun**.

## The Backyard: Extinguishing the Fire and Ice Stairway

Equip the lockpin gun to bypass the locked door on the right side of the cabin. Stepping outside reveals a grim scene: a vehicle has crashed into an icy ravine and is actively burning.

```

### Gathering Patio Supplies

Before approaching the flames, clear the immediate terrace:

1. Pull the weather tarp off the patio grill.
2. Pick up the **Fire Extinguisher** and the bottle of **Cleaning Oil**.
3. Sweep the snow drift beside the grill to uncover a locked metal chest.
4. Pour the cleaning oil over the chest's corroded lock mechanism so it can accept a key later.

### Solving the Ice Stairway Puzzle

To reach the burning vehicle safely, you must traverse an unstable ice flow. Tap the ice blocks to trigger the sliding puzzle. 

Your objective is to slide ice blocks into numerical sequence to form a stable descending staircase. Community reports highlight this puzzle as a common stumbling block due to its tight sliding lanes.

```

Slot 2: Block [ 2 ]
Slot 3: Block [ 3 ]
Slot 4: Block [ 4 ]
Slot 5: Block [ 5 ]
Buffer Lanes: Use top and side cutout slots to park non-sequential blocks.

### Vehicle Salvage Steps

Once the steps form a safe staircase:

* Use the fire extinguisher on the vehicle engine and cabin to suppress the flames.
* Search the two fallen syndicate members next to the wreck to obtain a **Crowbar** and a **Wrench**.
* Wedge the crowbar into the jammed driver-side car door to pop it open.
* Reach into the front seat to salvage the **Phone Cord**.

## Unlocking Hacksaw’s Phone and Mailbox

With the vehicle cleared, return to the cabin interior to extract Hacksaw's hidden digital communication logs. This segment of the **on thin ice chapter 1 walkthrough** links multiple physical items together.

```
Repair Sequence:
Wrench -> Wheelchair -> Small Key -> Oiled Box -> Screwdriver -> Prosthetic Leg -> Phone
```

1. Plug the salvaged phone cord into the wall socket beside the wireless charging pad.
2. Inspect the broken wheelchair in the corner. Use your wrench to tighten the loose axle bolts; this dislodges a **Small Key**.
3. Head back to the backyard patio and insert the small key into the oiled metal chest. Collect the **Screwdriver** and an additional set of **Triangles**.
4. Return to Hacksaw's body. Use the screwdriver to remove the screws holding his prosthetic leg compartment closed.
5. Take the drained **Smartphone** from the cavity.
6. Rest the smartphone on the active wireless charging cradle. After a brief narrative exchange between Kate and Murphy, take the charged device.
7. The device requires facial authentication. Hold the screen up to Hacksaw’s face to bypass biometric security.

Read through the unlocked message history. Hacksaw was receiving extortion demands from an unknown syndicate operative, and a recent supply drop was delivered directly to his front gate mailbox.

Go out to the front cabin driveway. Tap the high-security mailbox and use the unlocked smartphone to scan the digital release code. The door snaps open, granting you the final set of **Triangles** and an encrypted **Chart**.

## Cabinets: The Triangulate Puzzle and Secret Safe

Return inside the cabin and focus on the open upper wall cabinets. You now possess all necessary triangle tiles to complete the electrical circuit board.

### Triangulate Puzzle Mechanics

Place all collected triangles into the empty sockets on the cabinet board. The goal is to channel an electrical pulse from the input node on the left to the yellow receiving triangle on the far right.

* Each time the electrical current lands on a triangle, that specific tile rotates 90 degrees clockwise.
* Plan your path so that tile rotations do not sever connections to subsequent nodes.
* Tap the illuminated adjacent triangles in sequence to guide the pulse safely across the board.

Completing the electrical loop triggers a hidden catch, ejecting a photographic print (**Photo**).

### Uncovering Hacksaw's Hard Drive

Now you hold the three critical components needed to crack the final puzzle of the chapter:

1. The **Letter** (from the floor safe)
2. The **Chart** (from the exterior mailbox)
3. The **Photo** (from the cabinet circuit puzzle)

Approach the marked bulletin board on the cabin wall. Pin the letter, chart, and photo onto the designated mounting outlines. 

Aligning the grid points between the three documents reveals the release latch for a hidden wall safe behind the paneling. The safe swings open, revealing Hacksaw's backup external hard drive containing the ransomware decryption keys. 

Kate and Murphy secure the evidence just as sirens echo in the distance, bringing Chapter 1 to a close. Utilizing an **on thin ice chapter 1 walkthrough** ensures you preserve your hint stars for the even tougher puzzles waiting in Chapter 2.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the safe code in Hacksaw's cabin?
The safe combination beneath the cabin floorboards is **9416**. It is solved by counting the tattoo symbols on Hacksaw’s body according to the sequence shown on his paper slip: 9 Arrows, 4 Skulls, 1 Heart, and 6 Waves.

### How do I open the mailbox in front of the cabin?
You cannot pick or force the mailbox open. You must charge Hacksaw's phone on the desk cradle, hold it to his face to unlock it with Face ID, read the text message confirming the delivery, and then tap the mailbox with the phone in your inventory to trigger the digital unlock.

### Why won't the cleaning oil work on the patio box?
Player experience shows that many players forget to brush off the snow first. You must tap the snow mound next to the grill to expose the rusted latch before applying the cleaning oil from your inventory. Once oiled, you still need the small key retrieved by repairing the indoor wheelchair with your wrench.
