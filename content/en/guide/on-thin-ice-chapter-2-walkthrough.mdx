4. **Open the Security Box:** Examine the wall-mounted security panel beside the reception desk. Align the three dial symbols based on Lobby Bot's speech pattern prompts to unlock the panel.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the trick to the Lobby Bot chip puzzle in this on thin ice chapter 2 walkthrough?
The primary challenge of the Lobby Bot chip puzzle is matching the internal circuit lines. Every edge that touches another chip must mirror that chip's connector style (single trace, dual bus, or node). Anchor your layout by matching the flat-edged perimeter chips along the four outer boundaries first, which quickly limits where the remaining center pieces can fit.

### What happens if I choose the wrong dialogue option with Sara Stikowski?
If you select incorrect dialogue options (such as threatening her immediately with arrest or reaching for your weapon), Sticks becomes suspicious earlier, causing her to detonate the smoke grenade prematurely. While this does not result in a game over, following our verified dialogue tree guarantees you see every plot clue before she flees.

### How do I reach the key stuck in the office chandelier?
You cannot reach the chandelier key by tapping on it directly. You must first interact with the heavy velvet window curtains on the right side of the office to obtain the curtain rope. Drag the curtain rope from your inventory onto the chandelier to lasso and lower the key safely.

### Why won't the bookshelf safe open after placing all the items?
If the secret compartment fails to open, double-check the vertical position of the brass plaque and the desk lamp. Players frequently mix up the Level 4 lamp slot with the Level 2 fan slot. Compare your arrangement directly against our shelf placement matrix table above to ensure every relic matches its Polaroid counterpart.
