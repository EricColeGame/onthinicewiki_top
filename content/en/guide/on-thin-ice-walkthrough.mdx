## Chapter 1 Overview: Breach at the Precinct & The Cabin in the Woods

The story begins in the police station where Kate Gray's desk is cordoned off with crime scene tape following a cyber assault. Before meeting your informant, Hacksaw, you need to clear the workstation and establish your initial lead.

```
Station Desk -> Chip Reader (63849) -> Informant's Cabin -> Snowblower Assembly -> Keypad (40827)
```

1. **Investigate Kate's Desk:** Pick up the chip reader resting inside the evidence box on the floor.
2. **Access the Terminal:** Place the reader onto the desk next to the monitor. Inspect the chip reader closely to spot the bolded sequence: **63849**. Type this five-digit code into the terminal to unlock the computer.
3. **Arrive at Hacksaw's Cabin:** Kate and Murphy arrive at the remote hideout amidst heavy snowfall. Murphy discovers Hacksaw is not answering his phone after reports of a shootout.
4. **Repair the Snowblower:** You cannot reach the porch through the heavy snowdrift. Collect three components scattered in the front yard:
   * **Wheel:** Near the left fence line.
   * **Nozzle:** Beside the walkway.
   * **Fuel Canister:** Resting near the porch steps.
5. **Clear the Path:** Attach the wheel, nozzle, and fuel canister to the snowblower. Run the machine over the yard to clear away the snow drifts completely.
6. **Decipher the Cabin Keypad:** Pick up the loose metal number plates revealed beneath the snow. Place them onto the board fixture on the right wall. Aligning the numbers correctly across the top row reveals the sequence **40827**. Enter **40827** on the door keypad to gain entry to the cabin.

## Cracking the Informant's Safe: The Tattoo Decryption Puzzle

Inside the cabin, the scene turns grim. The informant has been fatally shot, and foul play is evident. You must catalog the room, uncover hidden compartments, and open the heavy floor safe.

```
Open Window -> Cut Floorboards (Hacksaw) -> Wash Safe -> Count Tattoos -> Unlock Safe (9416)
```

Start by clearing the clutter. Push aside the disheveled mattress and roll back the floor rug. Open the window shutter to bring natural daylight into the dark room. Collect the cloth curtains from the window frame. Open the wall cabinets to locate the locked mini-game compartment and pick up the metal hacksaw tool.

Examine Hacksaw's body across four distinct points of interest:
* Check his phone case to discover the phone has been taken or concealed.
* Inspect his prosthetic leg to identify a fastened panel.
* Collect the paper symbol strip lying close to his hand.
* Inspect his body tattoos closely.

Use the hacksaw on the loose, squeaking floorboards near the bed to expose a locked iron safe caked in dried blood. Step outside to the puddle created by melting snow, dampen the cloth curtains, and return inside to wash the blood away from the safe's dial.

As outlined in this **on thin ice walkthrough**, the safe requires a four-digit combination calculated by matching the symbols on the paper strip to the matching tattoos on Hacksaw's skin.

| Strip Symbol | Visual Description | Body Tattoo Locations | Total Count (Safe Digit) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Arrow** | Pointed directional arrow | Arms, neck, and chest | **9** |
| **Skull** | Classic skull icon | Forearms and torso | **4** |
| **Heart** | Anatomical/cardiac heart | Upper chest and shoulder | **1** |
| **Wave** | Water current ripples | Wrist and collarbone area | **6** |

Enter **9416** into the safe's numeric lock. Inside, you will collect:
* A confidential handwritten letter.
* A bundle of colored triangle tiles.
* A professional lockpin gun.

## Navigating the Backyard: The Flaming Car & Ice Stairway Puzzle

Equip the lockpin gun to pick the lock on the cabin's reinforced back door. Stepping outside reveals a chaotic crime scene: a car engulfed in flames and fallen gang members in the snow.

```
Extinguish Fire -> Search Bodies (Crowbar & Wrench) -> Solve Ice Stairway -> Open Car Door
```

Before you can safely inspect the vehicle, you must gather tools and extinguish the blaze:
1. Strip the weather tarp off the outdoor grill to find a **fire extinguisher** and a can of **cleaning oil**.
2. Clear the snow patch beside the steps to uncover a rusted lockbox. Apply the cleaning oil directly onto the rusted latch to prep it for later unlocking.
3. Approach the frozen ledge puzzle labeled the Ice Stairway.

In this stage of our **on thin ice walkthrough**, players often get tripped up by the Ice Stairway sliding mechanic. The objective is to slide numeric ice slabs across the grid until each numbered block rests directly in its matching numerical indentation.

```
[Target 1] <--- Slide Block 1 across buffer lanes
[Target 2] <--- Use spare buffer tiles to bypass blockers
[Target 3] <--- Lock final sequence to bridge the ledge
```

Once across, douse the vehicle fire with the fire extinguisher. Inspect the two bodies near the wreckage to retrieve a heavy **crowbar** and an adjustable **wrench**. 

| Hazard / Obstacle | Item Needed | Source Location | Immediate Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Blazing Car Fire | Fire Extinguisher | Under grill tarp | Extinguishes vehicle fire |
| Rusted Box Latch | Cleaning Oil | Under grill tarp | Dissolves heavy rust |

| Jammed Car Door | Crowbar | Body near vehicle | Grants access to phone cord |

Use the crowbar to pry open the vehicle's warped passenger door and retrieve a spare phone charging cord.

## Unlocking Hacksaw's Phone & The Mailbox Mystery

Continuing our **on thin ice walkthrough**, return inside the cabin to assemble your electronics and bypass Hacksaw's biometric security.

```
Fix Wheelchair -> Get Key -> Open Box -> Unscrew Leg -> Charge Phone -> Face ID Unlock
```

Use the wrench on the damaged wheelchair resting near the interior doorway. Tightening the bolts releases a **small key**. Walk back outside to the burnt car and use the small key on the oiled lockbox. Inside, you will discover a precision **screwdriver** along with a second set of triangle tiles.

Inside the cabin, use the screwdriver to loosen the screws on Hacksaw's prosthetic leg. Inside the hidden compartment lies his dead smartphone.

1. **Powering the Device:** Plug the charging cord into the wall outlet and place the dead phone onto the wireless charging station on the counter.
2. **Conversation Interlude:** Kate and Murphy converse regarding the syndicate while the battery charges up to functional levels.
3. **Biometric Bypass:** The phone features an active facial recognition lock. Select the phone from your inventory, aim it at Hacksaw's face, and authenticate the lock screen.
4. **Reading the Transcripts:** Browse through the message threads. The logs reveal direct death threats from the ransomware organization, along with confirmation that a critical package was left in the outdoor mailbox.

Step back out to the cabin's front driveway. Use the unlocked phone to transmit the digital signal code to the smart mailbox. Open the lid to claim the third set of **triangle tiles** and an encrypted **geographic chart**.

## The Triangulate Mini-Game & Uncovering the Hidden Safe

With all triangle tiles collected from the safe, the yard lockbox, and the mailbox, return to the wall cabinet inside the cabin. Insert the pieces to activate the Triangulate puzzle.

```
Insert Triangles -> Rotate Conduits -> Route Electric Dot -> Collect Crime Scene Photo
```

This mini-game requires you to navigate an electric current from the left power source over to the yellow target triangle situated on the far right. Every time the electrical charge moves across a tile, that tile immediately turns 90 degrees clockwise. 

* **Community reports** note that working backward from the yellow termination triangle helps visualize which orientations will connect properly.
* Avoid looping your current into dead-end circuits, as this forces you to reset tile angles.

Successfully guiding the current completes the circuit and drops a vital **photograph** from the secret cabinet partition.

| Clue Item | Acquired From | Placement | Final Discovery |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Letter** | Informant's Floor Safe | Pinned to Evidence Board | Deciphers encryption alignment |
| **Chart** | Front Driveway Mailbox | Pinned to Evidence Board | Maps file coordination nodes |
| **Photograph** | Triangulate Cabinet Puzzle | Pinned to Evidence Board | Completes trigger mechanism |

Affix the letter, the chart, and the crime scene photograph onto the investigation pinboard on the cabin wall. Arranging these three pieces exposes the hidden mechanical safe within the wall recess. Extract the encrypted hard drive containing Hacksaw's ransomware evidence.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the safe code for the cabin in the on thin ice walkthrough?
The cabin floor safe code is **9416**. You derive this number by dampening the cloth curtains in the outdoor puddle, wiping the blood off the safe door, and cross-referencing the symbol strip with Hacksaw's tattoos: 9 arrows, 4 skulls, 1 heart, and 6 wave symbols.

### How do I open the cabin front door if the keypad is not responding?
The front door requires you to find the loose metal numbers buried under the snowdrifts. First, locate the wheel, nozzle, and fuel can to fix the snowblower. Blow the snow away, grab the numbers, place them on the wall board to form **40827**, and punch that code into the keypad.

### Where are all the triangle pieces located in Chapter 1?
There are three separate sets of triangle tiles required for the cabinet puzzle:
1. Inside the blood-covered floor safe beneath the loose floorboards (Code: 9416).
2. Inside the rusted metal lockbox near the flaming car (opened using cleaning oil and the small key from the wheelchair).
3. Inside the front driveway mailbox (unlocked using Hacksaw's charged smartphone).

### How do you unlock Hacksaw's phone without a passcode?
After connecting the charging cord to the wireless pad and letting the battery power up, select the phone and tap directly on Hacksaw's face. The device uses facial recognition security, automatically granting access to his SMS records and the mailbox transmitter.
