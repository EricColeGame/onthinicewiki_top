## Core Mechanics: Understanding Light, Darkness, and Authority

The narrative of *On Thin Ice* revolves around your main character's internal healing and external reputation. Every significant dialogue option influences your primary statistics: **Light (😇)**, **Darkness (😈)**, or **Authority (🥇)**.

*   **Light (😇):** Represents emotional vulnerability, empathy, hope, and an earnest desire to heal from trauma. Building high Light allows you to overcome addictions and emotional paralysis.
*   **Darkness (😈):** Represents cynicism, raw self-preservation, emotional armor, and ruthless determination. High Darkness turns your character into an uncompromising fighter who demands retribution.
*   **Authority (🥇):** Measures respect, confidence, and control. High Authority changes how underworld bosses, coaches, and rivals treat you. Low Authority leads to public humiliation and loss of agency during dangerous confrontations.

Balancing Light and Darkness is generally not recommended if you want to hit the season's highest checkpoints. Community reports consistently show that players who specialize strictly in either pure Light or pure Darkness have a much easier time unlocking exclusive story achievements and passing high-cost stat gates.

## Key Decisions and Timed Choices in Episodes 1 to 5

The opening half of Season 1 tests your ability to react under pressure while introducing the core cast. Using this **on thin ice guide**, you can secure early Authority points and set up vital relationship boosts.

### Episode 1: Secret of the Ice
From the very first scene, prioritize standing your ground:
*   When coming to your senses: Choose **"Stood up"** (+1 Authority).
*   Handling Naomi taking your belongings: Choose **"Be careful, please"** for +1 Light, or **"Give it back now!"** for +1 Darkness. Selecting **"Where are your manners?"** yields +1 of both stats.
*   *Warning:* When reflecting on your actions, do not choose "I was stealing," as player experience confirms you will suffer a **-1 Authority penalty** later in Episode 3.
*   *Wardrobe Tip:* Purchasing the premium outfit (💎33) grants **+2 Authority** and earns immediate praise from Brian.

### Episode 2: The Dark Past
Episode 2 introduces high-stress timed prompts:
*   When challenged about your presence: Pick **"I'm better than any man"** under the timer to claim +1 Authority. Do not nod silently, or you will lose 1 Authority point.
*   **The Club Password (Timed):** Choose **"Wild duck"** on your first attempt. Selecting incorrect options like "Western king" or "White swan" forfeits the +1 Authority bonus.
*   *Path Differences:* On the Path of Light, the protagonist acts with quiet, modest restraint around Mr. Ocean. On the Path of Darkness, she acts openly hostile and crosses her legs aggressively.

### Episode 3: Free Me
Episode 3 introduces Hodge Baker and cements your standing with his brother Brian:
*   When greeting Brian: Choose **"Hug him"** (+1 Light) or **"High-five him"** (+1 Darkness).
*   **Reconciling the Brothers:** Spending 💎20 on "Here's a thought" awards +1 Authority and serves as the foundational choice for reconciling Brian and Hodge.
*   Skating choice: Picking the premium **"Axel jump"** (💎20) yields +1 Authority and starts accumulating your mastery skill.

### Episode 4: Back to Basics
*Warning: This episode handles traumatic subject matter regarding abuse.* 
*   **The Headmaster Confrontation:** You need **5 Authority points** to select **"(Grab the scissors)"**. This permanently scars the antagonist and asserts dominance. If you lack the points or scream, you suffer a stat reduction.
*   Timed prompt upon returning to the rink: Select **"Am rising from the ashes"** to gain +1 Authority.

### Episode 5: The Bitter Truth
Episode 5 demonstrates the power of cumulative choices. If you consistently supported the Baker brothers' reconciliation in earlier chapters, Hodge steps in to punish Headmaster Thompson legally and socially, earning you an automatic **+1 Authority**. If you neglected their bond, Brian refuses to turn to his brother, and Thompson escapes unpunished.

### Episode 6: The Shadow of My Soul
This chapter holds one of the strictest stat checks in the game:
*   Timed box moving: Choose **"Take two or three boxes at a time"** (+1 Authority).
*   Bar confrontation: Spend 💎23 to **"Stand up for yourself"** for +1 Authority.
*   **The Major Light Gate:** At the conclusion, the MC struggles with substance dependency. If you have accrued **28 Light points**, choose **"But I found the strength to resist."** This awards **+1 Authority** and unlocks the **Powerful Incentive** achievement (+20 diamonds).

### Episode 7: A Glimmer of Hope
Episode 7 focuses on information gathering. When speaking with Hodge, opt for constructive criticism instead of flattery; he respects honesty. Spending 💎11 to ask **"Why are they stalling?"** earns +1 Authority.

### Episode 8: The Power Struggle
Bartending and handling Dominic requires fast execution:
*   **Cocktail Recipe (Timed):** Choose **"Two juices, liqueur, and vodka"** on the first try (+1 Authority).
*   **Shaking the Drink (Timed):** When prompted, select **"More"**, and on the next prompt, select **"Enough"**.
*   **Interrogating Dominic (Timed):** Choose **"(Try to force him)"** on the first attempt (+1 Authority). Any other approach wastes valuable time.

### Episode 9: It's Time
Episode 9 shifts between multiple character perspectives during an intense warehouse rescue:
*   **Playing as Brian:** When searching the vehicle, select **"Inside the car seat"** on your first try (+1 Authority).
*   **Sam the Dog Mechanic:** If your relationship with Brian is strong, his dog Sam insists on coming along. Sam's presence prevents Brian's arm from being broken during the shootout, granting the **True Friend** achievement (+30 diamonds).
*   **Playing as Liz:** Choose **"Stunned him"** on the first try (+1 Authority).
*   **Protecting Brian:** Spend 💎18 to **"Found the strength to stand up"** to shield Brian from gunfire (+1 Authority).

## Frequently Asked Questions About On Thin Ice

### Can I balance Light and Darkness stats in Season 1?
While balancing stats is technically possible and unlocks a unique dialogue scene with the Shadow in Episode 10, it is strongly discouraged. Splitting your points will cause you to miss both the **28 Light check in Episode 6** and the **36 Darkness check in Episode 10**, locking you out of 40 achievement diamonds and crucial Authority gains.

### What happens if I fail the Authority check in Episode 4?
If you have fewer than 5 Authority points during the confrontation with Headmaster Thompson in Episode 4, you cannot grab the scissors. Screaming causes you to lose another Authority point, and your character will sustain greater trauma without leaving a permanent mark on her attacker.

### How do I stop Brian's arm from getting broken in Episode 9?
To protect Brian's arm during the warehouse raid, you must cultivate a high relationship with him throughout Episodes 1 through 8. If your bond is strong, his dog Sam senses danger, joins the mission, and intervenes to save Brian at a critical moment.

### Why should I consult an on thin ice guide before spending diamonds?
Using an **on thin ice guide** prevents you from wasting diamonds on cosmetic items that offer no stat bonuses while highlighting the specific premium choices—such as reconciling the Baker brothers or executing difficult ice jumps—that yield permanent Authority and Mastery points.
