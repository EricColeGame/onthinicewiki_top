## Step-by-Step Guide to Finding the On Thin Ice Locker Code

Once you open the holding room door, you will notice two lockers. The locker on the left opens readily without a numeric combination, granting you a roll of police evidence tape. However, the locker on the right is secured with a modern 5-digit electronic lock.

If you tap on the digital lock right away, Kate will comment that the interface is too dirty to read. To enter the **on thin ice locker code**, you must first select the bottle of hand sanitizer from your inventory and apply it across the keypad. This action clears away the grime and reveals a 5-digit entry panel alongside a prompt instructing you to review station visitor policies.

```
Keypad Display Order:
[ HANDS ] - [ NO ITEMS ] - [ SHIRTS ] - [ SHOES ] - [ MOUTHS ]
```

### Deconstructing the Visitation Rules

To calculate the numeric combination, return your attention to the framed "Visitor Rules" sign you knocked down from the hallway wall. Many players make the mistake of counting the officers present in the station (Kate, Murphy, Jacob, Keiko, and Dan). According to player experience and community reports, this is the most common reason players get stuck on this puzzle.

The puzzle prompt directs you to analyze a hypothetical visitation scenario described explicitly in the rules:

* **Rule Statement**: "Only one visitor is permitted per prisoner at any time."
* **Calculated Population**: Exactly two people exist in this scenario (1 prisoner + 1 visitor).

Now, match the capitalized prompt terms on the sanitized keypad against standard physical traits and the explicit directives outlined on the notice board:

| Metric | Scenario Context (2 Individuals) | Mathematical Calculation | Digit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **HANDS** | Each person possesses two hands | 2 individuals × 2 hands | **4** |
| **NO ITEMS** | Regulations strictly forbid visitors from carrying items | Zero permitted personal items | **0** |
| **SHIRTS** | Dress code requires all visitors and inmates to wear a shirt | 2 individuals × 1 shirt | **2** |
| **SHOES** | Footwear rules require a standard pair of shoes per person | 2 individuals × 2 shoes | **4** |
| **MOUTHS** | Each person possesses one mouth (rules specify quiet visitation) | 2 individuals × 1 mouth | **2** |

Entering the **on thin ice locker code** sequence **4 - 0 - 2 - 4 - 2** immediately disengages the electronic latch. Inside, you will find Dan's confiscated personal belongings, which serve as the primary catalyst for clearing the room.

## What to Do After Unlocking the Locker

Cracking the **on thin ice locker code** gives you Dan's belongings, but your objective is to clear out all three officers so Kate can conduct a one-on-one interrogation. Follow these exact inventory trades to empty the holding floor:

### 1. Reassign Keiko
Select **Dan's Belongings** from your inventory and hand them to Keiko. She will take custody of the items to begin cataloging evidence, leaving her desk clear. Inspecting the surface she just vacated allows Kate to collect the **Arrest Papers**.

### 2. Dispatch Murphy
Navigate to Detective Murphy. Hand him the **Police Evidence Tape** that you recovered from the unlocked locker adjacent to Dan's locker. Murphy departs immediately to secure peripheral crime scene cordons throughout the blizzard.

### 3. Move Jacob
With Keiko and Murphy gone, approach Officer Jacob. Present the **Arrest Papers** you collected from Keiko's desk. Jacob must process the formal documentation, prompting him to leave Kate completely unsupervised in the detention area.

### 4. Wake and Interrogate Dan
Now that the room is vacant, Kate needs to wake Dan from his stupor. Use the wire in your inventory to hook Dan's **Eyeglasses** and the **Tin Mug** through the bars of his cell:
1. Position the tin mug beneath the leaking pipe overhead until it fills with cold water.
2. Splash the water onto Dan to jolt him awake.
3. Hand Dan his eyeglasses so he can see clearly.

```
Dan's Interrogation Flow:
Wake with Cold Water -> Return Eyeglasses -> Learn of Syndicate Threats -> Receive Belt
```

Dan confesses that the cybercriminal cartel abducted his daughter, forcing him to plant the ransomware on Kate's computer in hopes of being apprehended by competent detectives. He then surrenders his belt. When inspected in your inventory, Kate uncovers a secret pocket housing a small brass key. This key unlocks the office closet containing the specialized field laptop, setting the stage for Chapter 4.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the exact on thin ice locker code in Chapter 3?
The exact **on thin ice locker code** is **40242**. It is derived from calculating the total hands (4), permitted items (0), shirts (2), shoes (4), and mouths (2) belonging to a hypothetical pair of one inmate and one permitted visitor.

### Why won't the locker keypad let me enter any numbers?
The keypad cannot be manipulated until you clean the surface. Pick up the bottle of hand sanitizer from the shelf in the detention corridor and apply it directly to the dirty locker lock. Once clean, the keypad will accept input.

### How do I get the holding room keys from Jacob?
You cannot ask Jacob directly for the keys. You must knock down the framed visitor rules on the wall to obtain a length of wire. Use the wire in your inventory to quietly fish the keyring off Jacob's belt loop while his back is turned.

### What do I do with Dan's belt after opening the locker and waking him?
After you wake Dan with water from the tin mug and return his glasses, he hands you his personal leather belt. Select the belt in your inventory and examine it closely. Tap the buckle and slide open the hidden compartment to retrieve the closet key.
