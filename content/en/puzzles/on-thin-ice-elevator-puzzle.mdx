## Step-by-Step Guide to the On Thin Ice Elevator Puzzle and Lobby Bot

Accessing the elevator begins with interrogation. When speaking with Lobby Bot, cycle through all four available dialogue prompts. While the robot appears dismissive, its speech synthesis chip leaks vital security codes disguised within corporate rhetoric.

Pay close attention to words capitalized or emphasized in the robot's vocal patterns: **FIRST**, **CENTRAL**, and **LAST**. These three keywords correspond directly to three specific geometric shapes required by the wall safe dial located near the lobby entrance.

### Solving the Reprogramming Circuit Board

Once the computer chips are recovered, insert them into the robot's primary mainframe to initiate the reprogram mini-game. This grid represents the core of the on thin ice elevator puzzle. 

The objective is to arrange all available chip tiles on the circuit board grid so that every edge connects seamlessly with the adjacent tile. Each chip features specific circuit trace lines, prong connectors, or colored pathways running to its north, south, east, and west edges.

```
       [ N ]
  [W]  [CHIP]  [E]
       [ S ]
  Rule: Edge connectors must align identical trace counts 
        with neighboring chips across all active borders.
```

Follow these structured strategies to complete the board efficiently:

*   **Isolate the Corner Pieces First:** Look for chips with flat, non-connecting edges on two perpendicular sides. These must occupy the outer perimeter corners.
*   **Trace the Bus Lines:** Follow double-line traces running horizontally; these generally bridge the center row of the circuit board.
*   **Check Pin Counts:** If an interior edge shows three copper leads, the adjacent tile's facing edge must also have three corresponding copper leads.
*   **Avoid Forcing Fits:** If an edge matches its neighbor on the right but clashes with the tile above it, rotate or swap the tile immediately.

Completing this sequence successfully reboots Lobby Bot's routing protocols, unlocking the express lift doors and granting Kate and Penny immediate clearance to the executive floor.

## Common Pitfalls and Troubleshooting the Elevator Access Sequence

Many players navigating Chapter 2 encounter sticking points during the transition between collecting lobby tools and powering up the elevator. Player experiences and community reports highlight several common stumbling blocks during the on thin ice elevator puzzle:

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where do I find the missing tool to unscrew the light switch?
The screwdriver is stored inside a red metal toolbox locked behind the hidden maintenance door in the lobby wall. To access it, move the large potted plant on the left side of the room to reveal a wall latch. Trip the latch to drop the hanging ceiling sculpture, pick up a sharp sculpture shard from the floor, and use that shard to pry open the maintenance compartment door.

### How do I decipher the Lobby Bot clues for the wall safe?
Talk to Lobby Bot and ask every available question. Listen closely to the words emphasized in all-caps: FIRST, CENTRAL, and LAST. Each sentence describes a corporate value linked to a geometric symbol on the wall safe. Match the first symbol to the left tumbler, the central symbol to the middle tumbler, and the last symbol to the right tumbler to unlock the safe and retrieve the copper wire.

### What should I do if the on thin ice elevator puzzle circuit board won't lock into place?
If the board does not automatically complete when all chips are placed, you have an inverted edge connection. Pull all non-corner chips off the grid and inspect the pin counts along the borders. Every outgoing line must match an incoming line of identical thickness and spacing on the adjacent chip. Pay special attention to the central T-junction tiles, which are easily reversed.

### Does the on thin ice elevator puzzle reset if I make an incorrect move?
No, the circuit puzzle does not reset or penalize you for incorrect tile placements. You can freely pull chips off the grid and rearrange them as many times as necessary without spending stars or restarting Chapter 2.
