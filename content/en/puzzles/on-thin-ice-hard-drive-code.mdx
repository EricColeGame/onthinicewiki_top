## Step-by-Step Walkthrough: Finding and Fixing the Suspect Chart

### 1. Repairing the Electrical Short
1. Tap on the bottom-right corner of the corkboard to inspect the faulty wiring.
2. Peel back the official police badge sticker covering the exposed electrical leads.
3. Grab the roll of electrical tape from your inventory (or nearby desk supplies) and patch the frayed wire.
4. Once taped, the electrical current stabilizes, illuminating three specific suspect profiles marked with colored backlights.

### 2. Translating Criminal Aliases into Numbers
Each highlighted profile features a street moniker that translates directly into a standard numerical value. Many players get tripped up here by looking for dates or badge numbers instead of basic wordplay:

* **Blackjack (Blue Frame):** In the card game Blackjack, the target hand value is 21. This gives you the blue value **21**.
* **Dimebag (Red Frame):** In street slang, a "dime" refers to the number 10 (or a ten-dollar bag). This provides the red value **10**.
* **Nooner (Green Frame):** A "nooner" is midday slang referring directly to 12:00 PM (noon). This yields the green value **12**.

| Suspect Moniker | Board Border Color | Slang / Context Meaning | Numeric Translation | Hard Drive Placement |

Aligning the numbers to match the drive's color ordering gives you **21-10-12**. Once input, the drive clicks open, allowing Kate to extract the internal hardware without triggering the drive's wipe security protocol.
