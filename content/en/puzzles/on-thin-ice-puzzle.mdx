## Frequently Asked Questions About On Thin Ice Puzzles

### How do I find the safe combination inside the cabin?
The safe combination is derived from the informant Hacksaw's tattoos. After cutting the loose floorboards with the hacksaw and cleaning the safe with a damp cloth, review the symbol strip found near his hand. Count the total occurrences of each symbol across his body: 9 Arrows, 4 Skulls, 1 Heart, and 6 Waves. Enter the code **9416** to open the safe door.

### What is the trick to solving the Ice Stairway puzzle without restarting?
The secret to the Ice Stairway sliding challenge is utilizing the two upper buffer spaces strictly for temporary holding rather than long-term storage. Always clear and position the lowest numbered steps (1 and 2) first. Once the base steps are locked into their correct resting tracks, cycle the remaining three tiles through the upper loop until they drop into descending order.

### Why won't the cabinet open for the Triangulate on thin ice puzzle?
If the cabinet puzzle cannot be interacted with, you are missing one of the three required triangle sets. You must unlock the floor safe (Set 1), open the rusted exterior box using the small key retrieved from the wheelchair (Set 2), and unlock the roadside mailbox using Hacksaw's facial-recognition-unlocked phone (Set 3). All sets must be placed on the board before the circuit activates.

### Can you bypass minigames in Adventure Escape Mysteries?
Yes. If you find yourself completely stuck on a puzzle, the game allows you to bypass the puzzle entirely by spending in-game stars. However, using the step-by-step strategies outlined in this guide allows you to conserve your stars for future chapters where puzzles become significantly more intricate.
