Her youngest son Kevin suffers from severe asthma and constantly needs expensive prescription medication. The financial squeeze turns critical when the family jalopy breaks down in the middle of a medical crisis. Desperate for immediate transportation to an emergency clinic, Patsy turns to her acquaintance Carrie.

Having emerged from the brutal withdrawal cleansed and determined to protect her children, Patsy attempts to walk away from distribution entirely. She visits Carrie’s home to announce her departure and offer support, only to walk into a gruesome crime scene.

Recognizing that Spider will eventually eliminate her and her children to plug potential leaks, Patsy contacts law enforcement. She agrees to wear a wire and lead police to Spider's hidden operation.
